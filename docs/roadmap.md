# One-year maintenance roadmap

Planning horizon: October 2026 through September 2027. These are intentions, not promised acceptance dates, adoption, staffing or new products.

- October–December: close genuine independent review, publish a correctly scoped RC, establish authenticated corporate program records, collect outside-checkout reproduction feedback, fix substantiated defects, maintain advisory/native-source review and bounded fuzzing. Preserve the two-message scope and offline file handoff.
- January–March: evaluate real additional fixtures and contracts, document compatibility/API decisions, improve coverage-guided fuzz seeds and supported installation profiles using measured results. Support upgrade instructions for actual published versions.
- April–June: evaluate stability and maintenance burden with actual contributors; consider broader platform support only after clean native builds, licensing and consumer tests. Maintain truthful release/signature/security evidence.
- July–September: review governance continuity and contributed corpus. Consider a stable public API only with independent review and external use evidence. Version 1.0 remains subject to its stated gates; no automatic promotion by date.

No payment execution, scheme rules, live APIs, custody, adapter execution, certification or sovereign rails will be added. camt.053 stays excluded unless redistribution rights and rigorous semantics are established through review. No future Aksum tools, hosting transfer or foundation relationship is announced here.
