# camt.053.001.08 rights decision — excluded from this release

**Decision: exclude the asset and do not implement or advertise camt.053 support.**
The zero-fee public-source review is complete for this release. A sufficient explicit
asset-specific redistribution basis was not established. This is a definitive repository
inclusion decision, not a claim that redistribution is legally impossible everywhere.

The general use grant is meaningful. The mandate nevertheless expressly excludes assets
whose redistribution rights remain uncertain. Neither an MIT license on a mirror's software
nor another project's attribution supplies missing contributor authority. We do not relabel
ISTH contributions as exclusively SWIFT-owned, and we do not silently relicense ISO assets.

Requirement status for bundled camt.053 support: **BLOCKED-BY-HUMAN**. The specific unblocker
is an authoritative written confirmation from the Registration Authority/rightsholders that
the exact unmodified schema may be redistributed with this Apache-2.0 software, identifying
required notices/conditions; alternatively a qualified human rights review may establish the
existing grant's applicability. No paid publication or service is required to request that
clarification, but no reply or permission can be generated by software.
